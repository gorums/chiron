# The 30-Hour Curriculum

Three parts. 19 modules. 30 hours. Each module is sized in minutes so you can fit it into whatever time you actually have.

**Suggested pace:** 2 hours/week → done in 15 weeks. 5 hours/week → done in 6 weeks. Do not do more than ~2.5 hours in one sitting; retention collapses after that.

---

## PART 1 — FOUNDATIONS (8 hours)

The part that does not expire. Channels change every 18 months; this does not.

| M02 | [Customers: segments, ICP, jobs-to-be-done](../01-foundations/M02-customers-and-research.md) | 90m | Build an ICP, run 5 customer interviews, find a real buying trigger |
| M03 | [Positioning, brand, messaging](../01-foundations/M03-positioning-and-messaging.md) | 90m | Write a positioning statement and a message hierarchy that survives contact with a customer |
| M04 | [Product, price, place — the offer](../01-foundations/M04-offer-price-place.md) | 60m | Set a price with a rationale, understand why distribution beats promotion |
| M05 | [Funnels, journeys, and unit economics](../01-foundations/M05-funnel-and-unit-economics.md) | 90m | Model a funnel, compute CAC, LTV, payback — and know when a channel is unaffordable |

## If you have less than 30 hours

**The 10-hour emergency version** (real literacy, no execution skill): M01, M02, M03, M05, M07, M08 (skim), M10, M13, M15.

**The 15-hour operator version** (you need to run something next month): all of Part 1 except M04, plus M07, M08, M10 or M11 (pick the one matching your business), M12, M13.

Do not skip **M05 (unit economics)** or **M13 (measurement)** under any circumstances. Marketers who cannot do arithmetic are the ones who get fired.
